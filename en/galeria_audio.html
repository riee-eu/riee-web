EUROPEAN ACADEMY OF JACA 2009

Talks and interviews with some of the participants in the 2009 European Academy of Jaca, in chronological order. The recordings are in Spanish.

  • Carlos Bastarreche: an optimistic view of Europe

  • Juan Fernando López Aguilar: Fighting Europhobia and promoting respect for people and their freedoms

  • Maximiliano Bernad: Thoughts on the image Spain is projecting in the EU

  • Juan Carlos Usero: Municipalities will be the ones to bring Europe closer to citizens

  • César Romero: Europe is not facing a crisis of values

  • Ana Gascón: The challenge Europe poses for young people

  • Enrique Villarroya, Mayor of Jaca, a city open to Europe

  • María Victoria Broto: Education against the crisis

  • Jaime Mayor Oreja wants a less virtual and more real Europe

  • Javier Elorza is confident that Europe will lead us out of the crisis
