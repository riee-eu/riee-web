5. Privacy policy

This website has no forms or registration areas, and it does not collect personal data from its visitors.

If you write to info@riee.eu, the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos will process the data you send us (your name, e-mail address and whatever you include in your message) in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD):

  • Controller: Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos, with the contact details given in section 1.
  • Purpose: to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, to manage your relationship with the Institute.
  • Legal basis: your consent when you write to us and, where applicable, the taking of pre-contractual steps or your relationship with the Institute.
  • Retention: for as long as necessary to deal with your enquiry and to comply with any applicable legal obligations.
  • Recipients: no data are disclosed to third parties, except where required by law. Messages are received and handled through e-mail service providers acting as processors, which may store the data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR.
  • Your rights: you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability by writing to info@riee.eu. If you consider that your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operational reasons, in accordance with its own privacy statement. The Institute has no access to those logs.

6. Cookie policy

This website does not set its own cookies or use any advertising tools.

Visitor statistics: to find out how many people visit the website and which pages they view, we use GoatCounter, a statistics service that does not set cookies or identify visitors. It only records, in aggregate and anonymous form, the page visited, the referring website, the type of browser, operating system and screen, and the country. It does not store IP addresses or allow a person to be tracked between visits. The data are hosted on servers in the European Union (Germany and Finland) and are not shared with third parties. More information in its privacy policy.

Some pages include third-party content that may set its own cookies when it is loaded or used:

  • YouTube videos (video gallery and some news items): they are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which does not set cookies until the user plays the video. More information in Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps (contact page and the "How to get there" page of the European Academy of Jaca): Google may set cookies when displaying the map. More information in Google's cookie policy.

The "Share on Facebook" button on the news pages is an ordinary link: it does not load anything from Facebook until it is clicked.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
