17/05/10 Registration opens for the European Academy of Jaca and the Course on International and European Civil Service and Diplomacy

Registration is now open for the 26th edition of the European Academy of Jaca and the second Course on International and European Civil Service and Diplomacy. Both will take place from 19 to 23 July in Jaca, the Academy in the afternoons and the Course in the mornings.

For more information:
- on the European Academy of Jaca, see: www.riee.eu/jaca_presentacion.html (in Spanish)
- on the Course on International and European Civil Service and Diplomacy, see: www.riee.eu/funcpubint_informacion.html (in Spanish)
