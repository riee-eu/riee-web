03/12/11 Conference "25 years of Spain in the European Union and the crisis"

On 14 and 15 December, from 5 to 9 pm, the conference "25 years of Spain in the European Union and the crisis" will be held in the former Salón de plenos (council chamber) of the Provincial Council of Zaragoza, with the collaboration of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos.

Speakers will include the renowned writer Leopoldo Abadía (author of "La Crisis Ninja") and Ambassador Javier Elorza, regarded as Spain's leading expert on the subject.

Registration is free and must be made in person on 9 or 13 December, from 5 to 7 pm, at the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos (San Jorge, 8, 1st floor).

A certificate of attendance will be issued, and attendance will count towards the Diploma in Advanced European Studies (in Spanish)
