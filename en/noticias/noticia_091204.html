04/12/09 Juan Fernando López Aguilar gives the inaugural lecture of the 22nd Master's Programme in European Union

On 4 December, Juan Fernando López Aguilar, Chair of the European Parliament's Committee on Civil Liberties, Justice and Home Affairs and former Minister of Justice, gave the inaugural lecture of the 22nd Master's Programme in European Union of the RIEE, on "The area of freedom, security and justice before and after the Treaty of Lisbon".

After meeting Marcelino Iglesias, President of the Government of Aragón, and the Minister for the Presidency, Javier Velasco, the ceremony took place in the Sala Jerónimo Zurita of the Government of Aragón, chaired by the Minister for Science, Technology and Universities, Pilar Ventura, and the Minister for Territorial Policy, Justice and Home Affairs, Rogelio Silva.

The 22nd class, which began its studies on 27 October, is made up of participants from 9 countries in Europe, the Americas and Asia, from ten different degree backgrounds.
