02/06/09 Registration opens for the 23rd Master's Programme in European Union

On 28 October the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos opens registration for the 23rd Professional Master's Programme in European Communities and European Union (October 2010 to June 2011), the oldest in Spain, organised in collaboration with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation and the European institutions.

It takes place in Zaragoza and includes intensive sessions in Brussels, Strasbourg, Luxembourg and Madrid. It is aimed at university graduates in any discipline, final-year students and people with equivalent experience, and is compatible with work. It includes courses on foreign trade, career guidance, and international and European civil service and diplomacy.

It is a professional Master's Programme with limited places, designed to train true European specialists in the legal, economic and business, political, historical and social fields, with a practical, realistic and multidisciplinary approach, taught by 90 senior officials of the Spanish and European administrations, Ambassadors, international civil servants, professors and other experts. Its graduates work in companies, bodies and public administrations in 32 countries, as well as in international organisations and in the European Union.
