16/01/15 Manuel López presents the Medal of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos to MEP and former Minister of Justice Dr Juan Fernando López Aguilar.

The President of the Conference of Rectors of Spanish Universities, Manuel López, presents the Medal of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos to MEP and former Minister of Justice Dr Juan Fernando López Aguilar at a ceremony held in the lecture hall of the Paraninfo of the University of Zaragoza

MEP and former Minister of Justice Dr Juan Fernando López Aguilar with members of the Ricardo Díez-Hochleitner class (27th Master's Programme in European Union) in the Paraninfo of the University of Zaragoza, shortly before receiving the Medal of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos.
