14/07/10 Master's Programme diploma ceremony at the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos

The head of the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, Jaime Caruana, presented the Master's Programme diplomas of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos on Monday 12 July at the headquarters of the Real Sociedad Económica Aragonesa de Amigos del País.
