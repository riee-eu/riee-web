12/04/11 Working sessions of the Master's Programme of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos with González-Páramo, member of the Executive Board of the European Central Bank

On Monday, at the Bank's headquarters in Frankfurt, José Manuel González-Páramo, member of the Executive Board of the European Central Bank, Antonio Sáinz de Vicuña and other senior ECB officials met the participants of the Master's Programme of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos to discuss the decision taken the previous week.
