06/07/09 Presentation of the 25th European Academy of Jaca at the City Council

Tomorrow, Tuesday 7 July, at 9.45 am, in the Salón de plenos of Jaca City Council, and chaired by the Mayor, Enrique Villarroya, the Rector of the European Academy of Jaca, Maximiliano Bernad, accompanied by one of the Vice-Rectors, Juan Carlos Usero, and by Luis Silván, Secretary-General, will present the 25th edition of the European Academy of Jaca, which will be held from 20 to 24 July at the Palacio de Congresos.
