30/05/11 His Royal Highness the Prince of Asturias received a delegation of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos on its 25th anniversary

His Royal Highness the Prince of Asturias has received a delegation of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos at the Palacio de La Zarzuela on the occasion of its 25th anniversary, which coincides with that of the Kingdom of Spain's accession to the European Communities.

The delegation, led by most of the Institute's Board, also included the members of the Prince Felipe class (2010-2011) of its 23rd Master's Programme in European Union, which is taking place in Zaragoza, with intensive sessions in Brussels, Frankfurt, Luxembourg, Strasbourg and Madrid.
