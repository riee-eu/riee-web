03/11/13 ECB Director-General Antonio Sáinz de Vicuña receives the Medal of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos

Frankfurt, 4th. At a ceremony held at the headquarters of the European Central Bank, the Director-General of its Legal Services, Antonio Sáinz de Vicuña, today received the Medal of the Real Instituto de Estudios Europeos in recognition of his long dedication to the process of European integration in posts of the highest responsibility.

At the end of the working sessions of the RIEE's Master's Programme at the European Central Bank, its President, Mario Draghi, sent a video message to the 26th class, of which he is the patron.
