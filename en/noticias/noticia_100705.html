05/07/10 Presentation of the 26th European Academy of Jaca and the 2nd Course on International and European Civil Service and Diplomacy at the City Council

Tomorrow, Tuesday 6 July, at 10 am, in the Salón de plenos of Jaca City Council, and chaired by the Mayor, Enrique Villarroya, the Rector of the European Academy of Jaca, Maximiliano Bernad, accompanied by one of the Vice-Rectors, Juan Carlos Usero, and by Luis Silván, Secretary-General, will present the 26th edition of the European Academy of Jaca and the 2nd Course on International and European Civil Service and Diplomacy, which will be held from 19 to 23 July at the Palacio de Congresos.

The programmes can be found here:
- European Academy of Jaca: www.riee.eu/jaca_presentacion.html (in Spanish)
