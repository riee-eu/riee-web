27/10/09 Sessions begin for the 22nd class of the Master's Programme in European Communities and European Union

The 22nd edition of the Professional Master's Programme in European Communities and European Union opens in Zaragoza on 27 October 2009, with participants from nine countries and from a range of degree backgrounds. The Programme will run until June 2010, including intensive sessions in Brussels, Strasbourg, Luxembourg and Madrid, as well as three complementary courses on foreign trade, international and European civil service and diplomacy, and career guidance.
